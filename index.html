Template discovery

Find experimentally determined proteins with sequence similarity to your target.

BLASTP runs on NCBI's external service. This app prepares and transfers your sequence; search execution and result availability are controlled by NCBI.
1
Run BLASTP

Compare your target against a protein database and inspect identity, query coverage, E-value and accession.

2
Choose a suitable template

Prefer good coverage and significant similarity. Check whether the template has an experimentally solved structure and appropriate biological context.

3
Retrieve the template structure

Use the PDB accession to inspect the structure and download coordinates from the RCSB Protein Data Bank.

Comparative modelling workflow

Continue with a selected template using a dedicated modelling platform.

This version does not generate 3D coordinates on its own. It prepares your sequence and links to external modelling tools; no model or validation score is fabricated.
1
Submit target and template to SWISS-MODEL

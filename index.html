01 · The concept

What is homology modelling?

Homology modelling, also called comparative modelling, predicts a protein's 3D structure by comparing its amino-acid sequence with that of a related protein whose structure has already been determined.

01 / TARGET

Target protein

The protein whose 3D structure is not yet known. Its amino-acid sequence is the starting point.

02 / TEMPLATE

Template protein

A related protein with a known experimental structure, often obtained from the Protein Data Bank.

03 / MODEL

Predicted structure

A 3D model built using the structural information from the template and the target-template alignment.

Key idea: proteins with a shared evolutionary history often retain similar structural features. A good template can therefore provide a useful starting point for predicting the target structure.

Why alignment matters

It connects sequence to structure

A reliable alignment maps target residues onto corresponding template residues. Errors in alignment can lead to incorrect placement of parts of the model.

What should you look for?

  • Sequence identity and similarity
  • Conserved functional residues
  • Coverage of the target sequence
  • Insertions and deletions, especially in loops

04 · Quality assessment

A model is a prediction — validate it.

After model building, structural quality checks help identify geometrical problems and areas that may need refinement.

Geometry

Ramachandran plot

Shows backbone phi (φ) and psi (ψ) torsion angles. It helps assess whether residues occupy sterically favoured conformations.

Conceptual illustration only — not a real model score.
Structure comparison

RMSD

Root mean square deviation measures the average positional difference between corresponding atoms after structural superposition.

A lower RMSD can indicate closer structural agreement, when comparisons are appropriate.
Stereochemistry

Clashes and bond geometry

Check for unrealistic atomic overlaps, bond lengths, bond angles and side-chain conformations.

Overall assessment

Use multiple checks

No single score proves that a model is correct. Interpret validation results alongside template quality, sequence identity and coverage.

06 · Take-home message

Why does homology modelling matter?

Protein structure is closely connected to protein function. When experimental structure determination is unavailable or difficult, comparative modelling can provide a useful structural hypothesis to guide further research.

Remember the essentials

A suitable template + a reliable sequence alignment + careful model building + thorough validation = a more trustworthy homology model.
